# Gamefilled Product Directions

Gamefilled should feel like one product, not a collection of repeated list pages. Every surface needs a distinct purpose.

## Product surface contract

### Notifications
Direct, personal and actionable.

Examples:
- someone followed you;
- a follow became mutual;
- someone replied to or mentioned you;
- a list invite or collaboration request;
- a security or account warning;
- an import/sync completed or failed.

Notifications should never become a copy of the social feed.

### Activity
A profile history of what one player did.

Examples:
- marked a game as Played;
- rated or reviewed a game;
- changed featured games;
- followed another player.

Activity is useful for identity and profile context, not urgency.

### Pulse
A focused personal dashboard, not an infinite feed.

Potential modules:
- what close friends are playing now;
- games gaining momentum inside the user's circle;
- mutuals who recently finished the same game;
- one strong recommendation rather than dozens of cards;
- important tracked-game updates.

## Standout feature candidates

### 1. Play Next
Turn the backlog into a decision tool.

Inputs:
- available time;
- preferred platform;
- desired mood or genre;
- estimated completion time;
- friends currently playing;
- personal rating patterns.

Output: a small ranked shortlist with an explanation for each choice.

### 2. Journey
A game-focused personal journal.

Possible entries:
- play sessions and progress;
- memorable moments;
- screenshots;
- spoiler-protected notes;
- completion date and final thoughts.

A Journey can remain private or become a curated public timeline.

### 3. Taste Match
Compare two users without reducing them to a follower count.

Signals:
- shared favourites;
- rating similarity;
- completed games in common;
- genres and companies with repeated interest;
- opposing opinions worth discussing.

### 4. Tracked Game Updates
Users can follow a game itself.

Useful updates:
- release date changes;
- new trailer or media;
- DLC, expansion, remake or remaster connections;
- platform/store availability;
- major company announcements when reliable sources exist.

These belong in Notifications only when explicitly tracked by the user.

### 5. Game Universe and Company Network
Expand the existing side drawers into navigable relationships:
- parent game, expansions, DLC, remakes and ports;
- developer/publisher history;
- recurring collaborations;
- chronological franchise and company timelines.

## Interaction language

- Default controls use a dark surface, restrained green edge and green active state.
- Solid green is reserved for irreversible or explicit commit actions.
- Avoid full pages when a dropdown, drawer, tab or focused panel gives a better experience.
- Avoid duplicating the same data in Overview, Activity, Notifications and Pulse.
- Prefer one meaningful recommendation or insight over dense generic grids.

## Suggested order

1. Notification mini-inbox and direct-event foundation.
2. Tracked games and game-update notification model.
3. Pulse dashboard using existing social and library data.
4. Play Next recommendations.
5. Journey entries.
6. Taste Match and richer social discovery.
7. Steam sync as an optional data source for the features above.
